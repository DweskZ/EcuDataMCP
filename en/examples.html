The problem, first

Real questions, cited data.

Ask a general AI assistant about Ecuador and it's common for it to invent figures with total confidence: there isn't enough Ecuador in the data it was trained on, and it has no way to verify anything in real time. Give it EcuDataMCP's tools and it stops guessing: it searches the official portal, cites the source, and the number it returns is real. These ten examples show that difference.

search_sismos

“What earthquakes has the Geophysical Institute recorded in Ecuador recently?”

Returns the IG-EPN's latest catalog: magnitude, depth, location, and review status (automatic or reviewed) for each event, with a direct link to the official bulletin.

See Sismicidad Ecuador live ↗ www.igepn.edu.ec ↗

And the rest of the universe

“How many active taxpayers does the SRI's RUC registry have right now?”

search_sri_datasets

The most recent registry records 2,904,355 active taxpayers, summing the TOTAL column, not counting rows.

sri.gob.ec/datasets ↗

“How many people collected IESS unemployment insurance last month?”

query_resource_data

June 2026: 2,561 beneficiaries, USD 836,716.99 in total, excluding the TOTAL: row embedded in the file.

datosabiertos.gob.ec (IESS) ↗

“What was the weekly Grade 1 cacao price in June 2026?”

preview_resource_data

USD 174.77 / 168.15 / 166.28 / 188.07 per quintal across the four weeks, reading only the most recent MPCEIP file, without stacking prior versions.

datosabiertos.gob.ec (MPCEIP) ↗

“What are the requirements for getting a passport?”

get_tramite_info

Requirements, procedure, cost, and estimated time, straight from the gob.ec portal, without browsing it by hand.

gob.ec ↗

“Who won a given public tender, and for how much?”

get_contrato_info

The full OCDS record: buyer, tender process, award, and final contract.

compraspublicas.gob.ec ↗

“How has consumer confidence evolved this year?”

get_indicador_bce

The indicator's time series, at whatever frequency and unit apply (monthly, for consumer confidence).

contenido.bce.fin.ec ↗

“Which companies have the best ROE in a given sector?”

search_ranking

A ranking across ~38 financial ratios (liquidity, leverage, profitability), filterable by CIIU code and year.

supercias.gob.ec ↗

“Which INEC surveys have downloadable microdata on employment?”

search_anda

The NADA/IHSN catalog shows exactly which surveys carry downloadable microdata, with the direct link.

anda.inec.gob.ec ↗

“What rules regulate a given activity, with an Official Register reference?”

get_regulacion_info

The regulation's detail, with a direct link to the Official Register PDF.

gob.ec ↗
