The problem, first

Real questions, cited data.

Ask a general AI assistant about Ecuador and it's common for it to invent figures with total confidence: there isn't enough Ecuador in the data it was trained on, and it has no way to verify anything in real time. Give it EcuDataMCP's tools and it stops guessing: it searches the official portal, cites the source, and the number it returns is real. These eleven examples show that difference.

search_sismos

“What earthquakes has the Geophysical Institute recorded in Ecuador recently?”

Returns the IG-EPN's latest catalog: magnitude, depth, location, and review status (confirmed or reviewed) for each event, with a direct link to the official bulletin.

See Sismicidad Ecuador live ↗ www.igepn.edu.ec ↗

And the rest of the universe

“How many active taxpayers does the SRI's RUC registry have right now?”

search_archivos

The most recent registry records 2,904,355 active taxpayers, summing the TOTAL column, not counting rows.

sri.gob.ec/datasets ↗

“How many people collected IESS unemployment insurance in June 2026?”

query_resource_data

June 2026: 2,561 beneficiaries, USD 836,716.99 in total, excluding the TOTAL: row embedded in the file.

datosabiertos.gob.ec (IESS) ↗

“What share of households with children under 5 lacks an adequate sewage disposal system, according to the ENDI?”

list_zip_contents → read_zip_member

ENDI 2025-2026: 24.8% nationally (95% CI: 23.1-26.6), with 8.6% in the Highlands against 36.4% on the Coast. Read from one CSV inside INEC's ZIP of 90 tables, without downloading the ZIP.

ecuadorencifras.gob.ec (ENDI R3) ↗

“What's the WTI oil price right now, per the Central Bank?”

get_bce_indicador_diario

USD 99.12 per barrel as of 27 September 2026 — the Central Bank's daily series (history back to 2015).

contenido.bce.fin.ec ↗

“What are the requirements for getting a passport?”

get_tramite_info

Proof of payment, a valid ID card, and — if it's a renewal — the previous passport or the loss report, straight from the gob.ec portal.

gob.ec ↗

“Who won the school-desks tender for Universidad Técnica Luis Vargas Torres, and for how much?”

search_contratos

Tito Marcelo Coral Guevara, for USD 130,100 via electronic reverse auction (March 2023).

compraspublicas.gob.ec ↗

“How has consumer confidence evolved this year?”

get_indicador_bce

The Global Consumer Confidence Indicator rose to 36.40 in May 2026, up from 36.07 in March — the BCE's monthly series.

contenido.bce.fin.ec ↗

“Which companies have the best ROE in a given sector?”

search_ranking

A ranking across ~38 financial ratios (liquidity, leverage, profitability), filterable by CIIU code and year.

supercias.gob.ec ↗

“Which INEC surveys have downloadable microdata on employment?”

search_anda

The NADA/IHSN catalog shows exactly which surveys carry downloadable microdata, with the direct link.

anda.inec.gob.ec ↗

“What rules regulate a given activity, with an Official Register reference?”

get_regulacion_info

The regulation's detail, with a direct link to the Official Register PDF.
